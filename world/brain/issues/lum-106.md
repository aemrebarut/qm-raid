LUM-106 (bug, severity 2) in [[components/onboarding]]: Invite emails arrive after the trial clock starts.

Reported by: [[companies/acme-robotics]] via [[people/maya-chen]], [[companies/orchard-education]] via [[people/june-park]]

Invited teammates get their email hours late, and the trial is already running when they join.

## Repro
1. Create a workspace and invite two teammates.
2. A billing receipt backlog is draining on the mailer queue.
3. Invites arrive hours later; the trial banner already shows fewer than 14 days.

## Where to look
`onboarding/invites` (enqueue) and `onboarding/trial` (when the trial clock starts).

## Done when
Invites are not stuck behind billing receipts, and the trial starts when the first invited teammate accepts.
