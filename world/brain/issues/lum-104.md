LUM-104 (bug, severity 3) in [[components/auth]]: SSO users are logged out every 10 minutes.

Reported by: [[companies/northwind-freight]] via [[people/lena-okafor]]

Since Northwind moved to SSO, dispatchers are bounced to the login page every few minutes.

## Repro
1. Workspace with SAML SSO whose identity provider clock runs 3 minutes fast.
2. Sign in, keep working.
3. After about 10 minutes the user is bounced to the SSO login page.

## Where to look
`auth/session_refresh` (expiry check) and `auth/saml_assertion` (NotOnOrAfter handling).

## Done when
Sessions refresh silently with the refresh token for the full session lifetime, even with IdP clock drift up to 2 minutes.
