# Team Art: make the board look great (Age of Empires quality, original assets)
Agents: raid-art-plan (lead), raid-art-units, raid-art-world, raid-art-fx (implementers), raid-art-rev (reviewer). Owns `packages/art/` (lead owns `packages/art/src/index.ts`, the API, and the showroom scaffold), `docs/lanes/art-plan.md`, `reviews/art.md`. Look at the live board first: http://127.0.0.1:4619 (test board on mock) and the current meshes in `apps/board/src/scene/` (owned by raid-ui-scene; read only).

Principle: microstructure. The art package is a library of procedural low-poly Three.js factories with no game logic. It never imports board code. The board's scene (raid-ui-scene) swaps its placeholders for art factories one by one behind a flag, so nothing breaks mid-swap. The board's vite config already allows imports from the repo root.

- raid-art-plan first step (now, within 8 minutes): read `apps/board/src/scene/*` and agree the API with raid-ui-scene (herdr prompt), for example `makeUnit({cls, teamColor, forged}) -> {object3d, play(anim: "idle"|"walk"|"work"|"cast"|"celebrate")}`, `makeBuilding(kind: "library"|"barracks"|"forge", {level}) -> {object3d, pulse(), glow(on)}`, `makeTarget({kind, severity}) -> {object3d, hit(), defeat()}`, `makeTerrain(size, zones)`, `makeProps(rng)`, `fx: {recallBeam(from, to), rememberOrb(from, to), sparkle(at), dust(at), flag(at)}`, `lighting(scene, renderer)` (sun, hemisphere, soft shadows, fog, tone mapping). Scaffold `packages/art` with a showroom: Vite on 127.0.0.1:4620 (`bun run showroom`) that displays every asset, animation and effect on a turntable, so each implementer can work and be reviewed in isolation. Write the plan, commit, tell the implementers and raid-ui-scene.
- raid-art-units (now): characters. Villager-scale low-poly people with readable silhouettes per class (knight: helmet, shield, staff; ranger: hood, bow-staff; scout: light cloak; forged River types: glowing runes in their type colour), team-colour tabards, idle bob, walk cycle, work (staff strikes), cast (staff raised, used for recall), celebrate. Monsters and enemy camps by kind and severity (bug = slime or goblin camp, feature = crystal or ruin; bigger and meaner with severity), hit and defeat animations.
- raid-art-world (now): buildings and land. Library as a stone monastery with a glowing window and a GBrain crest, Barracks in timber with banners, Forge as a smithy with a furnace, anvil, smoke and a River-blue glow, walls with gates and towers for each zone, terrain with gentle height, grass variation, dirt roads between zones and buildings, water or a river, trees, rocks, fences, crates, farms. Instanced meshes where counts are high.
- raid-art-fx (now): lighting and effects. Warm late-afternoon sun with soft shadows, hemisphere fill, light fog, ACES tone mapping, subtle ambient occlusion or outline for readability, selection rings, order ping, recall beam (blue, from Library to unit, with rising glyph particles), remember orb (gold arc into the Library, Library pulses), victory flag, dust on walk, Forge sparks. Keep 60 fps on a laptop: budget particles, no heavy post-processing.
