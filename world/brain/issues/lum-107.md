LUM-107 (feature, severity 2) in [[components/onboarding]]: Bulk invite teammates from a CSV.

Reported by: [[companies/brightpath-clinics]] via [[people/dev-patel]]

Clinic admins want to upload a CSV of staff and invite them all at once.

## Repro
Feature request; sample CSV has 40 rows, 2 duplicates differing only in case and trailing spaces.

## Where to look
`onboarding/csv_import` (validation) and `onboarding/invites`.

## Done when
Upload validates every row first (all or nothing), dedupes emails lowercase and trimmed, then enqueues invites on the mailer queue.
