LUM-101 (bug, severity 3) in [[components/billing]]: Payment retry double-charges a card.

Reported by: [[companies/acme-robotics]] via [[people/maya-chen]]

When the first charge attempt times out, the retry worker charges the card again. Acme saw two charges on one invoice. Check the retry worker and the charge client.

## Repro
1. Put a test card in the provider's slow mode (first call times out after 10 s).
2. Pay an invoice; the retry worker fires at 1 minute.
3. The card shows two charges for the same invoice.

## Where to look
`jobs/charge_retry` and `billing/charge_client` (how the idempotency key is built per attempt).

## Done when
A timed-out charge retried three times produces exactly one charge; a test covers the retry path.
