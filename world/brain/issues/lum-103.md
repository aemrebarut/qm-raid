LUM-103 (bug, severity 1) in [[components/billing]]: Invoice PDF shows the wrong currency symbol for CAD.

Reported by: [[companies/kestrel-labs]] via [[people/tomas-reyes]]

Kestrel is billed in CAD but the invoice PDF shows a plain dollar sign with no currency code.

## Repro
1. Account with currency CAD and locale en-US.
2. Download any invoice PDF.
3. Totals show `$1,200.00` with no currency code.

## Where to look
`billing/invoice_pdf` (currency formatting reads the account locale).

## Done when
The PDF shows `CA$1,200.00` (or `$1,200.00 CAD`) from the invoice currency, whatever the locale.
