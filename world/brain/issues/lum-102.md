LUM-102 (feature, severity 2) in [[components/billing]]: Prorated refunds on plan downgrade.

Reported by: [[companies/brightpath-clinics]] via [[people/dev-patel]]

Downgrading mid-cycle should refund the unused part of the plan instead of issuing a credit.

## Repro
1. Account on the Business plan, billed on the 1st.
2. Downgrade to Growth on the 16th.
3. Today: a credit note for the next invoice, no refund.

## Where to look
`billing/plan_change` (proration) and `billing/charge_client` (refund call).

## Done when
Mid-cycle downgrade refunds the unused days in integer cents, computed in the account's billing timezone; a retried refund is refunded once.
