## Art direction (the target, one screen)
We are leaving parchment-and-serif AoE2 pastiche for a 2026 strategy HUD: the world stays warm and saturated, the interface goes dark, thin and quiet so the world is the hero.
- Surfaces: slate glass. `--panel: linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(28,33,40,.94), rgba(15,18,23,.94))`, 1 px warm-metal hairline `--line: rgba(200,164,98,.42)`, inner highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.05)`, inner shadow `inset 0 0 24px rgba(0,0,0,.35)`, radius 3 px. No parchment, no paper texture, no drop shadows over 12 px.
- Ink: `--ink: #ECE6D8` primary, `--ink-2: #A7ADB4` secondary, `--ink-3: #6E757D` tertiary. Accents, one per system: GBrain gold `#D9A441`, River blue `#4FA7E0`, danger `#D65A45`, ok `#7CC47F`, team colours as the engine sends them.
- Type: headers `"Avenir Next Condensed","DIN Condensed","Roboto Condensed","Arial Narrow",sans-serif`, 600, uppercase, letter-spacing .08em, 11 to 13 px. Body `-apple-system,"SF Pro Text",system-ui,sans-serif` 13 px. Numbers `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. No serif anywhere in the HUD.
- Grid: 8 px. Buttons 32 px tall, command slots 56 px square, 8 px gaps.
- Icons: monoline SVG, 24 px grid, 1.5 px stroke, `currentColor`. Zero emoji, zero Unicode glyph icons (no ♞ ✉ 📖 ✨ 🪦 ⚔ →).
- Status: 8 px coloured pip + word in `--ink-2`; progress as 3 px bars. No pills.
- Motion: 140 ms `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)` on hover, open, close. Panels fade and slide 8 px.
- Voice: headers one or two words. Buttons one word + hotkey mark. No sentences telling the player what to click. Relative times (`12s`, `3m`). Human titles, never slugs, UUIDs, `river://` or epoch ids.
